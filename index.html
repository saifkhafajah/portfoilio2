01 // Profile

About Me

Engineer. Builder. Instructor.
#MakeOrange FabLab · Robotics Engineer

From schematic to working robot

I'm a mechatronics and R&D engineer focused on robotics systems, embedded control and custom PCB hardware. I've led robotic platforms from mechanical design and PCB layout through wiring, firmware, ROS integration and system testing.

My work spans a full-scale quadruped rover, a vision-guided robotic arm, indoor navigation, a drone flight-controller PCB and embedded-AI prototypes, built with Altium Designer, Fusion 360, SolidWorks, C/C++, Python, Raspberry Pi, NVIDIA Jetson Nano, ROS 1/2, Gazebo, RViz and OpenCV. I also teach hands-on PCB, CAD and embedded courses for IEEE, Orange and universities across Jordan.
